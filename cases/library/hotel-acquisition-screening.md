# Select-Service Hotel Acquisition — Blocked Pending Diligence

**Tags:** Blocked Judgment · Client / Professional Case

## The Question

A buyer was evaluating the acquisition of an operating select-service hotel in the United States. The real question wasn't a headline cap rate — it was whether the available seller materials actually supported a responsible underwriting decision, and what evidence could change the investment thesis.

## What the system was given

Seller-provided property and operating information, transaction assumptions, and supporting project materials — enough to begin professional screening, not enough to close a buyer-specific underwriting conclusion.

## What it found

The work spanned investment, underwriting, finance, legal/jurisdiction, market, and operations review rather than one free-form pass, and surfaced 11 material unresolved facts: incomplete operating history, unconfirmed buyer-specific financing assumptions, missing physical-condition and deferred-capex evidence, unresolved franchise-transfer and property-improvement obligations, open questions on competitive positioning, inconsistent property data, and legal/jurisdiction items needing further evidence.

## The judgment / result

**MORE DILIGENCE REQUIRED**, with underwriting status **BLOCKED** — not treated as a failure to answer, but as the decision the available evidence actually supported.

## What remained blocked, unresolved, or preserved

All 11 unresolved facts were kept visible rather than allowed to disappear into a narrative conclusion, connected through an Evidence-to-Decision Matrix to what each piece of future evidence would actually change: continue, reprice, restructure, request further diligence, reopen a workstream, or decline.

## What this case demonstrates

The system didn't manufacture a complete underwriting model because a buyer wanted an answer — it recorded what had to be known before a more definitive conclusion could be responsibly reached. This does not establish autonomous investment authority, universal expert-equivalence, or production readiness across every client, jurisdiction, or asset type.
