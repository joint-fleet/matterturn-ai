# Capabilities

MatterTurn's private foundation (judgment runtime, case evidence, orchestration) is not published here. What follows is the public-safe description of what that foundation supports, organized by maturity.

## Verified capability

- Structuring a professional decision around explicit evidence, assumptions, and risk.
- Tracing a conclusion back to the facts and assumptions it depends on.
- Distinguishing an irrelevant change from one that invalidates a specific assumption.

## Competition prototype (in progress)

- A cross-time judgment continuity adapter that takes a previous judgment and a new real-world event, and decides whether the judgment still holds.
- Publishing current judgment state to a public chain (Solana) as a shared, independently-readable status layer.
- See [`competitions/chiang-mai-build-lab-2026/`](../competitions/chiang-mai-build-lab-2026/) for the active build.

## Planned capability

- Automated, continuous monitoring of live evidence feeds against open judgments.
- Multi-domain judgment graphs where a single event can selectively reopen judgments across several professional systems at once.
