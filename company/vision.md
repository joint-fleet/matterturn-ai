# Vision

Reality doesn't stop changing after a professional makes a decision.

A property gets a new tenant event. A security's terms get reinterpreted after an event. A regulation shifts. Most systems — human workflows and AI tools alike — treat the moment of judgment as the end of the story. MatterTurn treats it as the start of one.

MatterTurn Ai builds judgment systems that keep professional decisions connected to changing reality: when new evidence arrives, the system asks not "should we alert on this?" but "does this materially change a judgment we already made?" — and if so, which parts of that judgment reopen, and which stay valid.

We are not building a general-purpose answer engine. We are building infrastructure for judgment continuity in domains where a wrong or stale decision has real professional and financial consequences.
