# Morocco Life Personal Assistant

**Maturity: Active engineering / validation.**

**Problem addressed:** Given a resident's or visitor's circumstances, what they need to know or arrange next in Morocco — documents, housing, transport, and everyday local services.

**Judgment supported:** Translating personal circumstances, location, and official requirements into a practical next-step guide, with uncertainties and official confirmation points shown rather than assumed away.

**Current public status:** In development. Not connected to live local-government or service-booking systems.

**What you can use this for today** (Active Engineering): check an official legal or regulatory question against primary government sources, with conflicts and version gaps preserved; determine whether two location records likely refer to the same real place, without ever auto-merging on proximity alone.

**What you actually get, by maturity:**
- *Active Engineering* — legal & regulatory research (tested against real official Moroccan government sources) and spatial/identity-boundary evidence matching (tested against a real public mapping service, 300+ passing tests in open, unmerged Draft work).
- *Research / Experimental* — broader everyday-service procedure guidance (housing, banking, transport, civil documents). Referenced in the project's planning record; not yet built and tested to the same bar.
