# Finance

**Problem addressed:** Whether a new event or piece of market evidence changes an existing financial or commercial judgment — and, in healthcare business contexts, which commercial choice is actually justified by available evidence.

**Judgment supported:** Binding evidence to a security or business decision at a specific point in time, testing competing explanations, and revising conclusions only where new facts require it.

**Current public status:** In development (securities) / design stage (medical business). Neither is a production trading, advisory, or clinical decision tool.
