# International Brand Strategy & Marketing

**Problem addressed:** Which market a product should enter, how it should be positioned there, and how the resulting plan should reach customers.

**Judgment supported:** Connecting local consumer voices, market comparison, competitive evidence, and culture/channel factors into a brand and marketing direction — separating raw observation from insight before recommending options.

**Current public status:** In development. Outputs are a reviewable direction with evidence gaps and assumptions visible, not a final, execution-ready marketing plan.
