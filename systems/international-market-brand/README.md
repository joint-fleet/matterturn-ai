# International Brand Strategy & Marketing

**Maturity: Active engineering / validation.**

**Problem addressed:** Which market a product should enter, how it should be positioned there, and how the resulting plan should reach customers.

**Judgment supported:** Connecting local consumer voices, market comparison, competitive evidence, and culture/channel factors into a brand and marketing direction — separating raw observation from insight before recommending options.

**Current public status:** In development. Outputs are a reviewable direction with evidence gaps and assumptions visible, not a final, execution-ready marketing plan.

**What you can use this for today** (Active Engineering — see the website's [market-entry research problem page](../../website/) for the fuller version): research a cross-border market-entry question against real, sourced evidence; track what's confirmed, contested, or missing as research proceeds; reopen only the specific part of a judgment new evidence actually affects.

**What you actually get, by maturity:**
- *Active Engineering* — market research & evidence capture, claim tracking & residual-need assessment, selective reassessment. Tested against three real market-entry research efforts (none has reached a completed commercial judgment); a minimal judgment runtime currently passes 64/64 tests in open, unmerged Draft work.
- *Planned / Not Yet Available* — consumer insight, market prioritization, positioning, naming, messaging, channel activation, go-to-market planning. Named in the system's own long-term scope; no engineering work has started.
