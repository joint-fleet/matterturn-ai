# Sales & Customer-Facing Decisions

**Problem addressed:** Frontline and customer-facing teams need to answer a customer's question with what can actually be confirmed right now, and know when to escalate instead of guessing.

**Judgment supported:** Separating what is confirmable from service information and guidance from what requires specialist review — spanning banking frontline service, traveler/travel-agency support, and cross-border sourcing and trade decisions.

**Current public status:** In development / synthetic testing, depending on the sub-system. None are connected to live production systems (bank accounts, booking inventory, or trading platforms).

**Link:** Product pages on the [website](../../website/) (`/systems/banking-frontline`, `/systems/travel`, `/systems/cross-border`).
