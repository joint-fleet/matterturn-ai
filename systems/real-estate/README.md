# Real Estate

**Problem addressed:** Whether to pursue a property investment or development opportunity, and what would change that decision later.

**Judgment supported:** Evidence-based investment review — tracing claims in asset documents and market evidence back to source, stress-testing assumptions and conflicts, and preparing the case for expert challenge before a decision is finalized.

**Current public status:** Internal validation. Not yet a client-facing production system; outputs are reviewable drafts, not final reports.

**Link:** Product page on the [website](../../website/) (`/systems/real-estate`).
