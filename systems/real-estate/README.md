# Real Estate

**Maturity: Advanced Engineering / Controlled Validation.** This reflects engineering verification — tests, CI, a formal runtime, one published case — not fully validated professional effectiveness, production readiness, or proven commercial ROI.

**Problem addressed:** Whether to pursue a property investment, development, or operating-asset decision, and what would change that decision as new evidence arrives.

**Judgment supported:** Evidence-based investment review — tracing claims in asset documents and market evidence back to source, stress-testing assumptions and conflicts, and preparing the case for expert challenge before a decision is finalized.

**Public-safe engineering evidence:** package-first canonical execution, nine professional capabilities, dynamic capability routing, tests and CI, a formal runtime/authority mechanism, and Japan/PLATEAU spatial-evidence research — each clearly labeled Formal, Candidate, or Research-only. See [`company/engineering-evidence.md`](../../company/engineering-evidence.md) for the fact-by-fact detail and what is explicitly *not* claimed yet.

**Public-safe case:** [`joint-fleet/real-estate-expert-system`](https://github.com/joint-fleet/real-estate-expert-system) has published one public-safe real-case result; see [`cases/`](../../cases/).

**What you can use this for** (each backed by real case or documented-capability evidence — see the website's [problem pages](../../website/) for the fuller version of each):

- Estimate a property's value under an acquisition model
- Analyze comparable rent for office, retail, or warehouse space
- Test debt capacity and debt-service coverage
- Test whether a refinancing or capital-recycling assumption holds up
- Review development cost and feasibility — teardown, renovate, or hold
- Review market demand and competitive positioning
- Prepare an underwriting conclusion — or learn why one isn't supportable yet
- Review operating performance for an income-producing asset
- Prepare an investment-committee-style synthesis across all of the above

**What you actually get.** One system, ten professional capabilities, one integrated judgment — not separate add-ons: investment analysis & problem diagnosis, asset valuation, underwriting, finance & debt analysis, development & cost review, market analysis, legal & regulatory review, operations review, model selection, and senior investment judgment.

**Maturity, capability by capability — not uniform.** A capability appearing inside a real case is not the same claim as that capability's own output being independently confirmed correct. 1740 Broadway's accept/reject direction matched the real historical outcome, but the valuation itself had a disclosed terminal-value defect and the model was denied promotion — so Asset Valuation, Finance & Debt Analysis (same model), Development & Cost Review (real but not yet independently human-reviewed), and Senior Investment Judgment (the synthesis inherits its inputs' weaknesses) are labeled **Engineering Validated**, not Case-Backed. Investment Analysis & Problem Diagnosis, Underwriting, Market Analysis, Legal & Regulatory Review, and Operations Review are **Case-Backed — Exercised in Real Case**, with no disclosed defect in their own output. Model Selection is Engineering Validated (exercised inside every case, no dedicated case of its own). See the website's [capability network](../../website/) for the full per-capability breakdown and reasoning.
