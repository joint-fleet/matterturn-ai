# Medical Business Decision System

**Problem addressed:** Which commercial choice in a healthcare business context is actually justified by the available evidence.

**Judgment supported:** Defining the use case and its boundaries, and identifying reliable sources and qualified reviewers for a bounded commercial — not clinical — decision.

**Current public status:** Design stage. This is a design direction, not a clinical or medical decision tool, and not yet connected to any production system.

**Link:** Product page on the [website](../../website/) (`/systems/medical-business`).
