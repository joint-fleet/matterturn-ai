# Systems

Public-safe index of MatterTurn's judgment systems, one entry per real system — not grouped into categories for directory tidiness. Each entry states the problem it addresses, the kind of judgment it supports, its current public status, and a link to more detail. Internal implementation, orchestration, and private case evidence are not included here.

- [`real-estate/`](real-estate/) — investment, development, and operating-asset decisions.
- [`financial-securities/`](financial-securities/) — securities investment decisions.
- [`bank-frontline/`](bank-frontline/) — banking frontline customer service decisions.
- [`travel/`](travel/) — traveler and travel-agency decisions.
- [`cross-border/`](cross-border/) — cross-border goods trade decisions.
- [`international-market-brand/`](international-market-brand/) — international market entry and brand strategy decisions.
- [`morocco-local-life/`](morocco-local-life/) — everyday life guidance for residents and visitors in Morocco.
- [`sales-opportunity/`](sales-opportunity/) — sales opportunity qualification (not yet publicly detailed).
- [`medical-business/`](medical-business/) — healthcare business decisions (design stage).

For the live, individually-maintained product pages, see the [website](../website/). For a published public-safe case, see [`cases/`](../cases/).
