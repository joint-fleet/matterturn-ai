# Banking Frontline Service

**Problem addressed:** What a bank service team can confirm for a customer's question right now, and what needs specialist review instead of a guess.

**Judgment supported:** Separating confirmable service information and bank guidance from genuine information gaps, and identifying the right escalation point.

**Current public status:** In development. Not connected to bank accounts or transaction systems.

**Link:** Product page on the [website](../../website/) (`/systems/banking-frontline`).
