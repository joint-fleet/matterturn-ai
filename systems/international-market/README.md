# International Market

**Problem addressed:** Which market a product should enter and how it should be positioned there, and — for residents and visitors abroad — what to know or arrange next given local circumstances.

**Judgment supported:** Connecting local consumer evidence, market comparison, brand positioning, and marketing execution across countries; and translating a person's situation plus local requirements into a practical, locally-grounded next step.

**Current public status:** In development. Neither sub-system is connected to live local-government or marketing-execution systems; outputs are reviewable directions, not final plans.

**Link:** Product pages on the [website](../../website/) (`/systems/international-brand`, `/systems/morocco-life`).
